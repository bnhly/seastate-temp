Sea state at Thylacine (Otway): wave height exceedance by month

Thylacine (Otway) lies in Bass Strait at 39.24° S, 142.90° E. Significant wave height there exceeds 2 m about 85% of the time across the whole year, roughly 26 days a month on average. The roughest month is August, with a mean significant wave height of 3.6 m and seas above 2 m about 95% of the time; the calmest is January, with a mean of 2.6 m and about 77% of the time above 2 m. Waves above 4 m occur about 18% of the time.
