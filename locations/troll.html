Sea state at Troll: wave height exceedance by month

Troll lies in the North Sea at 60.64° N, 3.72° E. Significant wave height there exceeds 2 m about 48% of the time across the whole year, roughly 15 days a month on average. The roughest month is January, with a mean significant wave height of 3.1 m and seas above 2 m about 77% of the time; the calmest is July, with a mean of 1.3 m and about 13% of the time above 2 m. Waves above 4 m occur about 9.0% of the time.

Wind at 10 m across the year: typically 7.9 m/s (15 kn), top 10% above 13.8 m/s (27 kn). Monthly means run from 6.1 m/s in July to 9.8 m/s in January.

Weather windows: a typical month fits about 13 separate 24 h periods with significant wave height below 2 m, fewest in January (about 4.8). Periods do not overlap, so a 30 day month holds at most 30.
