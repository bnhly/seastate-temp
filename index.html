Free historical sea state data for any offshore location. Click a point at sea and pick your months to see how often significant wave height goes over your limit, plus wind, wave period and direction, weather windows, current and tide, and hurricane, typhoon and cyclone history. The map also shows water depth and nearby oil and gas platforms, wells and pipelines. Based on more than 40 years of Copernicus records. Useful for early planning of marine and subsea work, with a one page PDF to share.
