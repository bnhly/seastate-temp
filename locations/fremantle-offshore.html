Fremantle offshore wave climate: wave height statistics, swell and 100-year Hs

Fremantle offshore lies off Australia at 32.05° S, 115.30° E. This page sets out its wave climate: wave statistics by month from the hindcast record. The average significant wave height over the year is 2.7 m. Significant wave height there exceeds 2 m about 73% of the time across the whole year, roughly 22 days a month on average. The roughest month is July, with a mean significant wave height of 3.3 m and seas above 2 m about 91% of the time. The calmest is January, with a mean of 2.2 m and about 63% of the time above 2 m. Waves above 4 m occur about 9.8% of the time. The 100-year significant wave height is about 9.7 m.

Data basis
