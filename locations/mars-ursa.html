Sea state at Mars-Ursa: wave height exceedance by month

Mars-Ursa lies in the Gulf of Mexico at 28.17° N, 89.22° W. Significant wave height there exceeds 2 m about 6.8% of the time across the whole year, roughly 2.1 days a month on average. The roughest month is January, with a mean significant wave height of 1.2 m and seas above 2 m about 11% of the time; the calmest is July, with a mean of 0.6 m and about 1.0% of the time above 2 m. Waves above 4 m occur about 0.2% of the time.

Wind at 10 m across the year: typically 5.8 m/s (11 kn), top 10% above 9.7 m/s (19 kn). Monthly means run from 3.9 m/s in July to 7.1 m/s in January.

Weather windows: a typical month fits about 28 separate 24 h periods with significant wave height below 2 m. Periods do not overlap, so a 30 day month holds at most 30.
