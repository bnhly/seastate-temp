These are long term climatological statistics and not a forecast. They are likely to underestimate extremes, especially close to coastlines, in sheltered or shallow water and in tropical cyclone conditions. The data is free for everyone to use, but it is not warranted by Thrust Maritime: use it at your own risk.

Free to use for planning purposes. Automated bulk extraction of the tool's data tiles is not permitted; the underlying open datasets are available from the original providers credited below and on the How it works page.

Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information: WAVERYS global wave reanalysis [1980-2023], 3 hourly significant wave height, peak period and direction. Wind statistics from the ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus C3S) on its 0.5 degree grid (wind, direction rose, diurnal cycle and ENSO split 1980-2024).
